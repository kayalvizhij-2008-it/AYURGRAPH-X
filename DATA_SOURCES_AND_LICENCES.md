# Data Sources, Licences, and Provenance Registry

## 1. Registry of Integrated Data Sources

| Source | Domain / Scope | Official Licence / Terms | Application Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PubChem (NCBI)** | Chemical structures, CIDs, SMILES, 2D properties | Open Access (NLM / NIH Public Domain) | Chemical identity resolution & property retrieval |
| **ChEMBL (EMBL-EBI)** | Bioactivity records (IC50, Ki, EC50), molecular targets | CC BY-SA 3.0 | Target bioactivity and binding affinity lookups |
| **GBIF (Global Biodiversity Information Facility)** | Species occurrences, taxonomic backbone | CC0 / CC BY 4.0 (attribution provided per dataset) | Species occurrence mapping & distribution visualization |
| **Kew Plants of the World Online (POWO)** | Botanical taxonomy, accepted names, synonyms | Kew Data Licensing / CC BY | Botanical synonym verification & family taxonomic hierarchy |
| **IUCN Red List** | Global conservation status categories | IUCN Terms of Use / Non-commercial research | Species endangerment and conservation status |
| **PubMed / NCBI E-utilities** | Peer-reviewed biomedical literature abstracts & PMIDs | US Gov Public Domain / NLM Terms | Scientific literature retrieval & evidence validation |
| **Open Targets Platform** | Target-disease associations and pathway annotations | CC0 1.0 Universal | Disease-target biological rationale verification |
| **IMPPAT (Indian Medicinal Plants, Phytochemistry And Therapeutics)** | Reference traditional use, formulation constituents | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (Non-commercial research only) | Verified baseline traditional names & documented formulations |

## 2. Ethical Data Principles & Responsible Traditional Knowledge
1. **Nagoya Protocol & Traditional Knowledge Attribution:** Traditional knowledge is explicitly credited to source cultures, communities, and historical pharmacopeias (e.g., Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Folk medicine).
2. **Anti-Biopiracy Safeguards:** AYURGRAPH-X is an academic exploratory research tool. It does not patent, claim exclusive rights, or commercialize indigenous traditional knowledge.
3. **Protection of Sensitive Biodiversity Coordinates:** Endangered or vulnerable species records from GBIF are generalized to administrative regions (e.g. state/country level) to avoid aiding illegal wild harvesting.
4. **Research Hypothesis Disclaimer:** AYURGRAPH-X generates testable molecular hypotheses for laboratory researchers. It NEVER provides clinical efficacy claims, medical diagnoses, or self-medication recommendations.
